2. Asynchronous model

Offscript works asynchronously. We do not hold live onboarding, discovery or status calls. You provide audio and feedback in writing.

Services are billed 100% upfront in US dollars. Delivery timing starts when payment clears and we hold usable audio. The Desk renews monthly. You can cancel with 14 days’ written notice before the next billing date.

4. Refunds

Fees are non-refundable once work has commenced.

5. 14-day credit

If you move from the Calibration Test or an Event Sprint to the Desk within 14 days of delivery, the fee you paid is credited toward month one of the Desk.

On full payment, we assign to you all rights in the delivered final written works. Offscript retains ownership of its underlying frameworks, prompts, systems and voice ledger templates.
