04 // CTO System Blueprint

The 14-Step Deterministic Engineering Architecture

Offscript Editorial is not a freelance writer with a prompt. It is a multi-stage software and human-in-the-loop pipeline with rigid failure boundaries.

STAGE 01

Sourcing & 4-Filter ICP Qualification (Steps 1–3)

ROLE 1: PIPELINE ANALYST

Automated Python ingestion monitors YouTube and technical podcast directories. Every target is evaluated against four non-negotiable gates:

1. Unit Economics: B2B SaaS founder / RevOps executive ($1M–$15M ARR).
2. Audience Arbitrage: >2,500 LinkedIn network with generic posting cadence.
3. Content Maturity: Infrequent, under-leveraged thought leadership.
4. Asset Quality: Unscripted interview, ≤900 views, ≥15 mins, 7–180 days old.
STAGE 02

10-Angle Extraction & Diarization (Steps 4–5)

ROLE 2: EDITORIAL AUDITOR

Raw audio streams are processed to isolate guest dialogue with zero host contamination. The engine extracts exactly 10 non-overlapping angles mapped across the 7 strategic lenses, with every angle hard-anchored to a verifiable timestamp and verbatim quote.

STAGE 03

Persona Cadence Synthesis & Outbound Assembly (Steps 6–7)

ROLE 3: GHOSTWRITER & SDR

Replaces rigid paragraph limits with authentic personality cadence: staccato 1-2 sentence tempo for aggressive operators, dense multi-sentence structured memos for systems architects, or narrative tension for visionaries.

STAGE 04

Deterministic 2-Pass QA Linting (Steps 8–10)

ROLE 4: QA AUDITOR

Two consecutive audit passes execute automated regex sweeps. Hard failure triggers: emojis, hashtags, em-dashes (—), en-dashes (–), AI transition tropes ("In today's fast-paced world", "Stop doing X. Start doing Y", "Here are 3 reasons why"), and formatting non-compliance.

STAGE 05

Human Approval, Precision Dispatch & RevOps CRM (Steps 11–14)

ROLE 5: REVOPS MANAGER

Strict human approval gate before cold delivery. Outbound dispatches are synchronized to prospect local morning inbox triage (8:00–8:30 AM) and logged automatically into the RevOps master database.
