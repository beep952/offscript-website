Why a 60% Enterprise Win Rate Is a Pricing Liability, Not a Victory

Early-stage founders often celebrate a 60% enterprise close rate as proof of product-market fit. In reality, closing six out of ten enterprise deals rarely means your product is irresistible. It means you are surrendering six-figure value at discount margins.

Healthy enterprise close rates sit between 25% and 35%. If seven out of ten prospects are not pushing back on contract size, you are not discovering your market ceiling—you are subsidizing buyers who had the budget to pay triple.

01 // The Protocol

Thought leadership should read like a boardroom memo, not a marketing brochure.

Most executive ghostwriting fails because agencies confuse volume with authority. When busy founders hand their voice over to conventional content shops, their hard-won operational lessons get flattened into cheerful listicles, breathless platitudes, and generic AI summaries.

Our role is to listen with an editor's ear: isolating your commercial friction, eliminating conversational filler, and preserving your authentic cadence.

Every recorded keynote, podcast appearance, or voice note is distilled through our rigorous Voice Extraction Protocol, turning unscripted convictions into boardroom briefs that command peer and buyer respect.

Principle I

Asynchronous by Design

Your calendar is your scarcest asset. We never ask you to sit through recurring 60-minute interview calls. Send us a podcast link, a recorded webinar, or a five-minute voice memo; our founding desk handles the rest.

Principle II

Thesis-Driven Rigor

We do not write surface-level recaps. Every executive memo is anchored in a clear commercial thesis—examining unit economics, architectural trade-offs, organizational design, or contrarian market truths.

Principle III

Uncompromising Restraint

Zero emojis. Zero hashtags. Zero breathless engagement bait. We write clean, muscular prose free of gimmicks—ensuring your public presence reflects the institutional caliber of your company.
