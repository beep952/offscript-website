The math

You have already paid for the thinking.

Most of what executive writing costs is time, not fees. Three costs are easy to miss.

The recording is already paid for

You spent an hour on that podcast or keynote. That hour is gone either way. Unwritten, it only reaches the people who found the recording. Written up, it keeps working in front of your network.

Meetings are the hidden fee

A weekly 60-minute briefing call is 4 hours a month and about 52 hours a year of your time, before you read a single draft. Here that number is zero.

Profiles get checked before contracts

Buyers, investors and candidates look you up before they commit. An inactive profile is a small, avoidable drag on every one of those moments.

The deal math. If your average contract is $25,000, one deal your writing helps win covers about seven months of the Desk. The $250 test is credited in full toward month one, so a trial costs you nothing if you continue.

We commit to deliverables and deadlines, not to revenue outcomes.

Questions

Practical details, answered plainly.

How do you write in my voice without interviews?

We work from your own recorded words. From the audio we map your sentence rhythm, vocabulary, examples and argument structure, then edit your strongest ideas into finished posts. You review the drafts asynchronously and your feedback calibrates the next batch.

How much time do I need to give you?

No scheduled calls. You send audio, then review drafts when it suits you. Most of your time is a few minutes of reading and a yes, a no, or a note.

How long does delivery take?

The Calibration Test and the Event Sprint arrive within 48 hours of payment and usable audio. Desk clients receive work on an agreed weekly set day, by 5 p.m. in their own time zone.

Can I buy more than one Event Sprint?

Yes. The $950 Event Sprint is per sprint, so you can run one for each keynote, launch or funding announcement. The $250 Calibration Test is limited to one per executive.

What if I do not have podcasts or keynotes?

Desk clients can send voice memos instead. We send a few short prompts and you answer them by voice note on WhatsApp or Telegram, about 10 minutes every two weeks, which gives us enough material for a steady publishing queue.

What is the 14-day credit?

If you move from the Calibration Test or the Event Sprint to the monthly Desk within 14 days of delivery, your full fee is credited to month one. That leaves a $3,250 balance after the $250 test, or $2,550 after the $950 sprint.

Who owns the writing?

You do. Once full payment is received, we assign all rights in the delivered final written works to you. We keep ownership of our own frameworks, prompts, systems and voice ledger templates.

Do you use AI, and is my audio kept confidential?

We use AI tools to transcribe and analyze audio. A human editor shapes and polishes every post. Non-public audio is kept confidential, and the commercial API tooling we use excludes client data from model training.

Do you post to my LinkedIn account?

No. We never ask for your credentials and we do not access your accounts. We deliver finished posts to your workspace and you publish them yourself.

Is this ghostwriting or thought leadership?

Both, in plain terms. We ghostwrite in your voice and you publish under your name. The thought leadership comes from your own recorded opinions and examples, so what buyers read is what you actually think, and you approve everything.
