Why a 60% Enterprise Win Rate Is a Pricing Liability, Not a Victory

Early-stage founders often celebrate a 60% enterprise close rate as proof of product-market fit. In reality, closing six out of ten enterprise deals rarely means your product is irresistible. It means you are surrendering six-figure value at discount margins.

Healthy enterprise close rates sit between 25% and 35%. If seven out of ten prospects are not pushing back on contract size, you are not discovering your market ceiling—you are subsidizing buyers who had the budget to pay triple.

01 // Our Philosophy

Thought leadership should read like a boardroom memo, not a marketing brochure.

Most executive ghostwriting fails because it confuses volume with authority. When busy founders hand their voice over to conventional content agencies, their hard-won operational lessons get flattened into cheerful listicles, generic industry summaries, and breathless platitudes that alienate sophisticated enterprise buyers.

Inspired by the editorial standards of premier B2B narrative studios, Offscript Editorial was built on a simpler premise: your sharpest thinking already happens out loud. When you debate a peer on a podcast, walk your leadership team through a post-mortem, or record a voice note after a customer call, you speak with candor, nuance, and conviction.

Our role is not to invent opinions for you. Our role is to listen with an editor's ear—isolating your most defensible commercial insights, structuring them into cohesive arguments, and polishing every sentence until it carries the unmistakable cadence of your leadership.

Principle I

Asynchronous by Design

Your calendar is your scarcest asset. We never ask you to sit through recurring 60-minute interview calls. Send us a podcast link, a recorded webinar, or a five-minute voice memo; our editorial desk handles the rest.

Principle II

Thesis-Driven Rigor

We do not write surface-level recaps. Every piece is anchored in a clear commercial thesis—examining unit economics, architectural trade-offs, organizational design, or contrarian market truths that earn peer respect.

Principle III

Uncompromising Restraint

True executive authority never begs for attention. We write clean, muscular prose free of gimmicks, engagement bait, or artificial clichés—ensuring your public presence reflects the caliber of your company.
