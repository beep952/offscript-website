04 / Questions

Practical details, straight answers.

How do you capture my voice without interviews?

We use unscripted audio where your thinking is already expressed—podcasts, keynotes, panels, town halls, or voice notes. Editorial work focuses on your arguments, vocabulary, rhythm, and examples, with your review providing the final calibration.

How much time do you need from me?

There are no scheduled content interviews or recurring calls. You share source material and review drafts asynchronously. The time required depends on the scope and the amount of feedback you choose to provide.

What is the 14-day retainer credit?

If you move from either fixed-scope pilot or event sprint to the monthly desk within 14 days of delivery, the full initial fee is credited toward month one: $250 or $950 respectively. Confirm the credit and eligibility in your engagement terms.

Who owns the work, and how is source material handled?

Ownership, confidentiality, and data-handling commitments should be confirmed in the applicable terms before source material is submitted or work begins. We recommend sharing only material you are authorized to provide.

Is this ghostwriting?

It is editorial collaboration built around your ideas and source material. You remain the subject-matter authority and final approver; we help shape the narrative and writing.

Can I start with one project?

Yes. The Cadence Calibration Test and Event Sprint are fixed-scope, one-time engagements. The monthly desk is an option when ongoing support makes sense.
